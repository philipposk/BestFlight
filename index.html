Find the best flight, not just the cheapest

BestFlight searches several flight sources at once and ranks the results by what matters to you: cheapest, fastest, or lowest carbon. It can also tell you whether a fare is likely to rise or fall, and watch a route for you.

What it does

  • Searches several flight providers at the same time and combines the results
  • Ranks flights by price, travel time or carbon footprint
  • Finds money-saving connection options, with clear warnings about the risks
  • Predicts whether a price will go up or down and suggests buying now or waiting
  • Watches a route and notifies you when the price drops
  • An AI assistant answers flight questions
